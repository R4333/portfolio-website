Aletheia

Built for legal teams who have to sit through court depositions. It transcribes the recordings with speaker labels, reads tone and speech patterns, watches the video for behavioural cues, and flags contradictions and evasive answers. The heavy lifting runs in background jobs with live progress, because depositions are long.

A desktop app that sits in on live sales and support calls. It transcribes both sides as the call happens, shows sentiment, objections and intent as they come up, gives a rough close probability, and writes a summary with action items when you hang up.
